# Adoption And Operating Model

## Current Understanding

Adoption and operating model covers how teams introduce, govern, and scale AI-assisted development. The local scope includes team conventions, skill files, training material, workflow selection, approval boundaries, human accountability, team structure, and operating agreements.

This topic owns practice adoption and operating-model decisions. It links upstream for product and vendor context while keeping local material focused on how humans and agents work together.
