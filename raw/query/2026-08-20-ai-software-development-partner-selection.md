# Query Fragment: AI Software Development Partner Selection

## Query Asked

What are the top ten factors that are important when choosing a software development partner for an AI solution?

## Answer Summary

Choose a partner by evidence of workflow and business fit, relevant delivery experience, AI architecture judgment, measurable evaluation practice, security and governance controls, data and integration competence, senior delivery capability, maintainability and knowledge transfer, transparent operating economics, and trustworthy commercial and support terms. Use a paid discovery or pilot with representative data and explicit acceptance gates to validate claims.

## Durable Concepts Detected

- AI development partner selection is a cross-cutting procurement and delivery-governance decision.
- Vendor demonstrations and generic model benchmarks should be replaced by representative workflow evidence and explicit acceptance thresholds.
- The selection rubric should cover both build capability and the customer's ability to operate, audit, maintain, and replace the resulting solution.
- Total cost should include data work, model and tool use, evaluation, review, monitoring, support, and change—not only implementation fees.
